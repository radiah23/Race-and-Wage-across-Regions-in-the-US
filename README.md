Co-authored this case study with Mercer Mercer, Catherine Weeks and Nafisa Mohamed. 

The United States Census Bureau, starting in 1940, has conducted a monthly survey of the labor
force known as the Current Population Survey (CPS). The CPS is used to provide current estimates
of the economic status. In this Case Study, we aim to address the question. Were Black males paid
less than non-Black males in the same region, with the same levels of education and experience?
The motivation behind this question is to determine how racial inequality in the labor force is
reflected in wage gaps across the regions in the US.
